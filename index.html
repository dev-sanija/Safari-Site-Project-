The residents of Yala

Meet the truly
untamed locals.

Yala holds one of Earth’s richest wildlife mosaics. Swipe or scroll through a few of its remarkable residents.

Panthera pardus kotiya

Sri Lankan Leopard

Yala has one of the world’s highest leopard densities, yet every sighting still feels impossibly rare.

Elephas maximus maximus

Asian Elephant

Family herds cross ancient corridors between Yala’s forests, tanks and open plains.

Melursus ursinus

Sloth Bear

An elusive forest wanderer with a remarkable nose for termites and seasonal palu fruit.

Crocodylus palustris

Mugger Crocodile

Ancient and patient, muggers bask at lagoon edges before slipping silently below.

Pavo cristatus

Indian Peacock

At dawn, their calls carry across the scrub long before the iridescent display appears.

Axis axis ceylonensis

Spotted Deer

Watchful herds often provide the first alarm when a leopard moves nearby.

Bubalus arnee

Wild Buffalo

Powerful silhouettes gather at waterholes, caked in cooling mud beneath the afternoon sun.

215+ recorded species

Exotic Birds

Painted storks, bee-eaters and eagles make Yala a year-round theatre for birdlife.
